Founder

Aid Rawlins

Founder, CommsPartner

Aid founded CommsPartner in 2006. From 2009 to 2024 he built, launched and led Mazuma Mobile Australia, pioneering phone buy-back in Australia and taking the business to market leadership.

Earlier, as a director of GEOS Alliance, he helped build an international emergency response coordination centre, later acquired by Garmin. Today he works as a mentor, non-executive and investor across technology, reuse and eCommerce businesses.

Our ethos

Doing good while doing business.

Good business and sustainability are not opposites.

Mottainai (もったいない) is a Japanese expression meaning “What a waste!” or “Waste not, want not”. It carries a real regret at wasting, misusing or failing to appreciate the full value of resources, objects, time or talent.

It is the thinking behind everything we do: find the value others overlook, and put it back to work.
