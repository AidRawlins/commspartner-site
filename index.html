ITAD - ITAM & Secure Data Erasure

CommsPartner manages APAC IT Asset Disposition (ITAD) and IT Asset Management (ITAM) directly and through our partners to deliver Secure Certified Asset Management:

  • Data Erasure – Data Shredding – Data Destruction.
  • Hard Drive HDD & Solid State Drive SSD Destruction.
  • Corporate and SME IT and Smart Phone Disposal.
  • IT Asset Audit, Deployment, and Redeployment.
  • Ex-Lease Remanufacturing & Remarketing.
  • IT and Phone Repairs for Reuse or Erasure Enablement.

It Pays to Recycle through our IT Recycling Services – Quick and Easy; Simply Email an Approximate Equipment Inventory from which an anticipated value can then be established or guidance on safe disposal given. We then coordinate the collection logistics or onsite erasure if needed.

Once your equipment is received our team will complete a “Test and List Audit” where all inventory is inspected, tested, and listed into a confirmed Inventory Listing, and Sell Order quote is given for Erasure, and a Return or Sell Order completion at the confirmed value and terms.

Our IT recycling services allow you to increase the financial return on your original investment by selling your Working and Faulty end-of-life, or surplus devices. This includes business computer recycling, computer and laptop disposal services, Smartphone and Tablet recycling, as well as asset disposal and advice.
